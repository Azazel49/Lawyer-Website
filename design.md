# Lawyer Website Design Specification

## Overview
Professional website for an advocate/lawyer in Kanpur, India. Modern, clean design with Tailwind CSS.

## Pages Structure
- Home (index.html)
- About
- Services
- Blog/Case Studies
- Contact

## Homepage Layout

### Header
- Logo/Brand name
- Navigation: Home, About, Services, Blog, Contact
- Responsive hamburger menu for mobile

### Hero Section
- Full-width banner with professional imagery
- Tagline: "Your Trusted Legal Partner in Kanpur"
- Call-to-action button: "Schedule Consultation"
- Contact information prominently displayed

### About Section
- Two-column layout
- Image of the lawyer/office
- Brief bio and credentials
- Key statistics (years of experience, cases handled, etc.)

### Services Section
- 4-6 service cards in grid layout
- Criminal Law, Civil Law, Family Law, Corporate Law, Property Law, Cyber Law
- Each card with icon and brief description

### Testimonials
- Client testimonials in carousel format
- Professional and trustworthy appearance

### Contact Section
- Contact form (Name, Email, Phone, Message)
- Office address, phone, email
- Map integration (Google Maps)

### Footer
- Quick links
- Social media icons
- Copyright notice
- Newsletter signup

## Technical Requirements
- Mobile responsive design (Tailwind CSS)
- Fast loading
- SEO optimized
- Form validation
- Contact form integration (EmailJS or similar)
- Google Analytics integration

## Color Scheme
- Primary: Navy Blue (#1E3A5F) - Trust and professionalism
- Secondary: Gold (#D4AF37) - Prestige
- Accent: Light Gray (#F5F5F5) - Clean background
- Text: Dark Gray (#333333)

## Typography
- Headings: Inter or Poppins (modern, professional)
- Body: Open Sans or Roboto (readable)
